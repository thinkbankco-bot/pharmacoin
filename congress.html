Which PACs

Political action committees connected to pharmaceutical and biotech drug manufacturers and their trade groups. Not insurers, PBMs, pharmacies, distributors, hospitals or device makers. The full list is below so you can audit it.

What it doesn’t include

Donations from individual employees, lobbying spend, super PACs, leadership PACs and dark money. The real number of industry dollars around a member is usually larger than what’s drawn here.

What it doesn’t mean

PAC contributions are legal and publicly disclosed. A contribution is not a bribe and doesn’t prove anything about a vote. It’s a receipt. Read it, then read the votes.
