Case file · public record

He kept a diary.

Dr. Anthony Fauci typed a journal for twenty years: a “Historical Record” from 2001 to 2015, then the pandemic diary. In July–August 2026 the U.S. Senate released 1,606 pages of it, plus a 136-page file on his prize money. PHARMA Holdings plc has reviewed the document with the seriousness it deserves. Below: the lines that stop the scroll, the body count he logged by hand, the press hits he tallied, and every day, readable in full.
