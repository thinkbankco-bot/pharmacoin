context

Scary words, normal science

Minigenome and reverse-genetics systems, pseudotyped surrogate viruses, animal models, antigen expression, nanoparticle immunogens, rodent ecology. All of it sounds like a movie trailer. None of it, by itself, is evidence of gain-of-function, engineering or release.
